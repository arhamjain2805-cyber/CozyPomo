
# CozyPomo

A cozy, **100% offline** Pomodoro focus timer for Android featuring animated study companions, frosted-glass customization, ambient audio, and gamified daily goals—built with zero ads and zero accounts required.

---

## Download (Android APK)

1. Go to the [Latest Release](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/%5BYOUR_USERNAME%5D/cozypomo/releases/latest) page.
2. Under **Assets**, tap `CozyPomo.apk` to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.
4. If Android asks, tap **Settings** and allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager, then tap **Install**.

> **Note on Play Protect:** Because CozyPomo is distributed directly outside the Google Play Store, Android may show an unverified-app prompt during installation. CozyPomo runs completely offline on your device and does not collect or transmit personal data.

## Features

| Category | What's Included |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Focus Timer** | Customizable focus and short-break lengths, plus a 4-block cycle with an automatic 30-minute Long Break |
| **Ambient Sound & Music** | Built-in Rain, Fire, Waves, and Hum ambient sounds, plus local MP3 playlist playback and optional break vibration |
| **Animated Companions** | Start with 5 companions (*Mochi*, *Pip*, *Bambo*, *Rusty*, and *Hooty*) and unlock up to 20 animated companions with break-time hydration animations |
| **Frosted-Glass UI** | Set any custom photo from your gallery as a full-resolution wallpaper behind a translucent interface |
| **Rewards & Marketplace** | Earn Coins for focus blocks and Rubies for Priority Tasks to unlock 12 themes, 12 frames, 20 companions, and a Streak Shield |
| **Tasks & Targets** | Track up to 3 Priority Tasks, unlimited Normal Tasks, a locked Daily Cycle Target, Calendar deadlines, and a Birthday Spin Wheel |
| **Offline Backup** | All progress is stored locally on your phone with one-tap **Export / Import Backup (.json)** |

---

## Privacy & Permissions

- **100% Offline:** No sign-up, no tracking, and no internet connection required to use the app.
- **Minimal Permissions:** Only **Storage** permission is enabled (used for picking custom wallpapers, playing local MP3 tracks, and exporting/importing JSON backups). Camera, Microphone, and Location permissions are disabled.
